
Coffee Cookies 
Buttery cookies infused with aromatic coffee notes and chocolate chips, delivering a rich roasted flavor with a satisfying sweetness.
Best paired with: Espresso — its bold, intense flavor enhances the coffee aroma while balancing the buttery sweetness.

Matcha Cookies
Fragrant green tea cookies with a delicate earthy taste, subtle bitterness, and a sweet finish, complemented by chocolate chips.
Best paired with: Cappuccino — creamy milk softens the matcha's earthy bitterness and creates a smooth, comforting combination.

Macadamia Cookies
Golden, buttery cookies filled with crunchy macadamia nuts, offering a delicate nutty aroma and a rich, creamy taste.
Best paired with: Americano — its clean, slightly bitter finish balances the buttery richness and highlights the nutty flavor.

Chocolate Cookies
Rich cocoa cookies loaded with chocolate chunks, combining a soft, crumbly texture with deep chocolate flavor.
Best paired with: Espresso — the concentrated coffee notes complement the chocolate's richness and balance its sweetness.

Strawberry Cookies
Sweet, fruity cookies with a lovely strawberry aroma and a gentle tangy note, creating a bright and playful treat.
Best paired with: Cold Brew — its smooth, mellow character complements the fruity notes without overpowering them.

Lemon Cookies
Lightly sweet cookies infused with fresh lemon aroma and a refreshing citrus tang, leaving a clean, zesty finish.
Best paired with: Iced Americano — its crisp coffee profile balances the sweetness and complements the bright citrus notes.
